VII. Semantic Search & Vector Retrieval KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH_INDEXING

At the core of the system is a high-performance vector database. Every profile is converted into a 1536-dimensional vector that captures the nuances of a candidate's skill set. This allows for "Concept-Based" searching—meaning a recruiter can find candidates whose experience aligns with the *meaning* of a job description, even if the specific keywords don't match.
